refs bookmarklet

Drag this to your bookmarks bar (if you have an older + refs there, replace it):

+ refs
  1. Open any paper's PDF in your browser (publisher PDF, signed CDN link, Wiley/JPE/T&F/AEA viewer, arXiv, NBER, SSRN, or an author's homepage) — or its article page — and click + refs.
  2. 1-click PDF capture: refs reads the PDF straight from your browser's own cache/session (signed in as you, through your library proxy) and uploads it directly into your library folder. No downloading to Downloads, no Enter.
  3. A small refs window confirms the PDF arrived, follows identification and renaming, and closes itself automatically. Even if a publisher blocks popups or sets strict cross-origin policies, the PDF still lands straight in your library.
  4. On a paywalled article page (before opening the PDF), with a library proxy set under settings, refs opens the page through your proxy so you can sign in and click + refs again.

The bookmarklet carries a per-Mac key so only your own bookmark can upload directly to refs. After updating refs, drag + refs to your bookmarks bar again.
