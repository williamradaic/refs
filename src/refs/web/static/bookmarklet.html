refs bookmarklet

Drag this to your bookmarks bar (if you have an older + refs there, replace it):

+ refs
  1. On a paper's page (a journal such as the AEA's or OUP's, NBER, arXiv, an author's site) or on a PDF open in the browser, click + refs.
  2. It reads the page's citation details and, if your browser can open the PDF (open access, or signed in through the library), takes the PDF from the page itself. No downloading, no Enter.
  3. A small refs window shows the paper being added, and closes itself when it is done. The PDF lands in your quick-add folder, renamed; you can close the window early, the add carries on.
  4. Paywalled? With a library proxy in the settings (UC Berkeley: https://libproxy.berkeley.edu/login?qurl=), refs opens the page through it: sign in if asked, then click + refs again. Without one, refs looks for an open copy, and otherwise saves the paper without a file; save the PDF into the quick-add folder later and it attaches itself.
  5. Some journals (OUP's, for one) serve the PDF from another site, which the page may not read. Open the PDF and click + refs on it.

The bookmarklet carries a key that belongs to this Mac's refs, so only it, not some other website, can add papers without your Enter. Another browser or Mac needs its own copy from its own refs.
