refs bookmarklet

Drag this to your bookmarks bar:

+ refs
  1. On a paper's page (NBER, arXiv, a journal, an author's site, or a PDF open in the browser), click + refs in the bookmarks bar.
  2. A small refs window opens with the page's address in the add bar. Press Enter.
  3. refs finds the paper, downloads the PDF when it is openly available, and saves it in your quick-add folder (papers-dump unless you chose another). A paywalled paper is saved without a PDF; it attaches itself when you download it into that folder later.
